Application queue

How it works. "Prepare" opens a real Chrome window, fills the employer's form from your profile, attaches the tailored resume (Word) and cover letter, answers the demographic and screening questions you preconfigured, then stops so you can check it and press Submit yourself; the dashboard notices the confirmation and marks the role Applied. With auto-submit on, roles that meet your rules are submitted without review, up to the daily cap. Supported: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable career sites. Workday, LinkedIn and Indeed need an account and are not automated.

Career sites checked directly

International organizations (IMF, World Bank, UN Secretariat, UNDP, UNHCR, WFP, WHO, ILO, IDB, EBRD…), consulting firms and government contractors come from the organizations catalogue in config.yaml, which also fixes their sector; everything else is found automatically from the "apply at employer" links Indeed and LinkedIn return. All are checked at the start of every sweep. Titles go through the same filters as the big boards; only new postings have their descriptions fetched. Cornerstone and Taleo sites give title, location and link only. Change a site's sector here and its stored roles are re-tagged.
